Why we play

OUR AIM

For Mumtaz Girls School

The ISC Intra Sportsfest is more than a celebration of sports, teamwork, and competition; it is an opportunity to make a difference. This year, we aim to raise funds for Mumtaz Girls School, supporting girls who face financial barriers to education. Through our contributions, we hope to empower these hardworking students to pursue their dreams and build brighter futures. Together, let us transform the spirit of sports into a force for hope, opportunity, and lasting change.
